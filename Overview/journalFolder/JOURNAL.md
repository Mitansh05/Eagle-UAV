# October 25: Fixed Wing UAV

So today, I got started by researching about fixed wing UAVs. And the key topics that I researched were: what are fixed wing UAV, some real life examples, and what are the components of an UAV. The research log can be seen in the `Overview` folder. And the main point of doing the research was to know what I need to make for it to be even called a fixed wing UAV. It basically helped me by giving me the list of the components that I need, real life examples, and also gave me the basic definition of what a fixed wing UAV is, which cleared everything that I needed to know.

Overall all I did in this session is research which in my opinion is probably one the most important step of the design process, which what I am loosely following, as this is what decides what the final project be based around.

And after this step of the design process I am now thinking of starting the planing part of the design process. In which I will finally draft how the prototype will look which is also a critical part of the design process.
